# SportsCards

Use the local SportsCards MCP tools for the collection when connected.
Read [MCP setup and tools](../../../docs/MCP.md) when tool configuration or schemas are needed.
Read [the workflow](../../../docs/WORKFLOW.md) for dashboard operations and data limits.
Read [pricing assumptions](../../../docs/PRICING_ENGINE.md) before interpreting simulations.

The MCP server does not access eBay accounts.
Use compatible browser tools and the user's own signed-in session for authorized marketplace work.
When browser tools are unavailable, continue local work with supplied evidence and state the specific remaining dependency.
Do not infer account access from a local MCP connection.

## Physical cards and photos

- Treat a card record as one physical card and an observation as one view.
- Link repeated photos and front/back views to the existing card when the evidence supports the match.
- Keep distinct physical copies separate, including exact duplicate variants.
- Check player, year, set, number, parallel, print run, autograph, memorabilia, and raw or graded state.
- Treat detection and OCR as proposals. Preserve unclear identity fields for review.
- Distinguish glare, sleeves, and image artifacts from supported defects.
- Use the owner's current condition confirmation or an applicable earlier statement. Ask only when condition remains materially unresolved.
- Do not infer professional grade, authenticity, provenance, or an unseen surface defect.

## Purchases and evidence

- Preserve source references, dates, currency, quantity, refunds, and actual recorded costs.
- Leave missing purchase costs unknown. Do not replace them with market estimates or zero.
- Explain any estimated allocation across a lot. Keep unconfirmed purchase matches unresolved.
- Inspect the actual source before recording a reviewed comparison.
- Separate direct quotations, observed facts, owner statements, estimates, and simulation assumptions.
- Record source URL, observation or sale date, currency, buyer shipping, and exact-match rationale.
- Keep completed sales separate from active asks. Do not treat a disappeared listing as a sale.
- Do not infer an undisclosed accepted-offer price from the displayed asking price.
- Exclude incorrect variants, lots, stale evidence, and unsupported currencies from incompatible calculations.
- After an identity correction, review evidence that depends on the previous identity.

Use the user's authorized access. Respect access restrictions.
This workflow does not require bulk scraping, hidden endpoints, paywall bypass, or extracted browser credentials.

## Analysis and listing preparation

Keep sold estimates, asking scenarios, purchase costs, and unknown values distinct.
State effective price, sale-probability, fee, and shipping assumptions with simulation results.
Do not describe scenario percentiles as a validated forecast or calibrated confidence interval.

Prepare complete drafts for the selected cards within the user's scope.
Use one reviewed front photo and one reviewed back photo of each physical card.
Resolve local validation blockers before describing a draft as ready.
Keep private purchase costs and internal price guidance out of buyer-facing text.

Draft preparation does not authorize marketplace publication.
Reuse explicit authorization that still covers the cards, terms, and action. Do not request it again without a material change.
When required details or authorization are missing, complete the local draft before requesting the missing information.
After publication, verify the resulting listing and its terms before reporting it as live.
After an uncertain submission, inspect the account before retrying to prevent duplicates.

## Private data

Keep photos, purchase records, browser sessions, account data, research captures, and backups outside the public repository.
Use synthetic data in documentation, tests, and public issue reports.
Report verified work, estimates, unresolved evidence, and untested behavior separately.
